Why is reading books important

Knowledge and learning

Books are a good source of information and help provide insight into various topics.

Preservation of culture and history

Books preserve various cultural traditions and historical events.

Personal growth

Reading books helps in mental growth and acquisition of knowledge.

Escapism and entertainment

Fiction allows readers to explore new worlds.

Communication and connection

Books can facilitate communication and understanding between people from diverse background.

Mental stimulation and cognitive benefits

Regular reading helps reduce mental stress and improve mental well-being.

BBA subject books and best books for BBA students

Business Mathematics for all Courses Paperback : first January 2014 by D.C. Sancheti (Author)

Business Mathematics by D.C. Sancheti is a best resource for students across various business programs, providing a solid foundation in business mathematics. Its strengths lie in its broad coverage & practical focus, though it may not delve deeply into advanced topics.

B.Com (Hons) - Sem - 3: Business Mathematics Paperback – 1 January 2013 by R.S. Soni (Author)

The textbook "Business Mathematics" by R.S. Soni, published on January 1, 2013, is tailored for B.Com (Hons) students in their third semester. This paperback edition includes a variety of readings and activities aimed at enhancing students' comprehension and response skills.


Fundamentals of Statistics-Volume II Paperback – first January 2016 by A.M.Gun , M.K Gupta , B Dasgupta -author

Fundamentals of Statistics Volume two by A.M. Gun, M.K. Gupta, & B. Dasgupta is a good resource for those who have foundation in statistics & wish to advance their knowledge. It is clear explanations and comprehensive coverage & practical focus make it a useful textbook for students & professionals aiming to deepen their understanding of advanced statistical methods.

Modern Microeconomics Paperback : 20 May 2003 by A. Koutsoyiannis (Author)

This book offers a comprehensive exploration of both traditional and contemporary microeconomic theory, focusing on the behavior of fundamental economic agents: consumers and producers. The primary emphasis is on oligopoly, reflecting its prominence in the modern industrial landscape. The updated Second Edition introduces a third section that delves into three critical areas: factor pricing theory, general equilibrium theory, and welfare theory, ensuring that it encompasses all topics typically found in textbooks on price theory.


Computer Fundamentals :Pradeep K. Sinha : BPB Publications Paperback : 31 Dec 2010 by BPB Publications (Author)

Pradeep K. Sinha Computer Fundamentals is designed to introduce readers to essential concepts in Cs students. It serves as an ideal textbook for various introductory courses in IT and Computer Science, thanks to its clear, straightforward explanations and simplicity.

Management Information System Paperback – 1 December 2010 by Gupta A.K. (Author)

This book is designed for a diverse range of students. Marketing Management - sixth Edition Paperback: 16 Nov 2019 by Rajan Saxena

It is organized into three main sections: the first covers the basics of entrepreneurship, including its definition, types, and the factors driving its emergence. The second section delves into business planning and project report preparation, along with functional plans.


Research Methodology: A Guide for Researchers in Management & Social Sciences Paperback : 1 Jan 2006 by Taylor and Bill

This book is designed as a comprehensive text for postgraduate students in commerce, management, and social sciences, and is particularly valuable for first-time researchers. It covers a wide range of research methods used in management and social sciences, with a focus on practical application.

The book provides detailed guidance through every stage of the research process, from selecting a research project to finalizing a dissertation, report, or article. It organizes research methodology into three key dimensions: research strategies, research methods, and research data.

Healthcare Management New bookText & Cases Paperback : 1 Jan 2011 by S. K Sarangi

The book Healthcare Management: Text & Cases explores business management theories while focusing on healthcare management practices. It aligns with the syllabi for BBA(H) 6th Semester, BHM and MHM programs at WB Technical University and other institutions across India.
